Our Story

More than coffee.

NOIR & BEAN was built around one idea: a café should slow you down, not speed you up. Every detail, from the beans we roast to the music we play, is chosen to hold a moment a little longer — quality coffee, honest food, and a room that feels good to sit in.

It's a place for early meetings, long afternoons, and evenings that run past closing. Coffee, food, music and conversation, gathered under one warm light.
